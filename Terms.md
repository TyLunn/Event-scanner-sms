# SMS Terms & Conditions

**Event-Driven Opportunity Scanner**

Last updated: September 8, 2026

## Program Description

The Event-Driven Opportunity Scanner provides automated SMS alerts concerning publicly traded stocks and market events. Messages may include stock tickers, significant price movements, opportunity classifications, and brief descriptions of conditions detected by the scanner.

Messages are informational only. The service does not execute trades or transactions.

## Consent to Receive Messages

SMS messages are sent only to recipients who have expressly requested and consented to receive Event-Driven Opportunity Scanner alerts.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

## Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on market activity and qualifying scanner events. Some periods may have no messages, while periods of significant market activity may result in multiple messages.

## Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless carrier and service plan.

## Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP**. After opting out, no additional scanner alerts will be sent unless the recipient subsequently provides consent again.

## Help

Recipients may reply **HELP** for assistance or contact the sender directly for support.

## Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## Privacy

Mobile information and SMS consent information are handled according to the Event-Driven Opportunity Scanner SMS Privacy Policy. Mobile information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

## Informational Use

Market alerts are generated from automated data analysis and may contain errors, delays, or incomplete information. Alerts are not guarantees of market performance or investment results. Recipients are responsible for their own investment decisions.
