# SMS Privacy Policy

**Event-Driven Opportunity Scanner**

Last updated: September 8, 2026

## Information We Collect

We may collect a recipient's name and mobile telephone number for the purpose of providing requested Event-Driven Opportunity Scanner SMS alerts.

## How We Use Mobile Information

Mobile information is used solely to provide requested market-alert messages, service-related communications, and administration of the messaging service.

## Mobile Information Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent information, and opt-in data will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information may be provided to service providers solely as necessary to operate and deliver the messaging service.

## SMS Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on market activity and qualifying scanner events. Some periods may have no messages, while periods of significant market activity may result in multiple alerts.

## Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless carrier and service plan.

## Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying **STOP**. After opting out, no additional scanner alerts will be sent unless the recipient subsequently provides consent again.

## Help

Recipients may reply **HELP** for assistance or contact the sender directly for support.

## Consent

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

## Data Protection

Reasonable measures are used to protect information maintained for purposes of providing this messaging service.
